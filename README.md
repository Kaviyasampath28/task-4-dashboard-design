# Superstore Sales & Profit Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of **Task 4 -- Dashboard Design** of
the Data Analyst Internship.

The objective is to design an interactive dashboard for business
stakeholders using sales and financial data. The dashboard was developed
in **Microsoft Power BI** using the Sample Superstore dataset.

## Business Objective

The dashboard is designed to help stakeholders:

-   Monitor overall sales and profitability performance
-   Track sales growth over time
-   Compare performance across regions and categories
-   Identify high-profit customers
-   Analyze sales by shipping mode and sub-category
-   Support data-driven business decisions

## Dataset

**Dataset:** Sample Superstore

The dataset contains sales-related information including:

-   Order Date
-   Customer Name
-   Category
-   Sub-Category
-   Region
-   State
-   Ship Mode
-   Sales
-   Profit
-   Quantity
-   Discount

## Dashboard KPIs

The dashboard includes the following key performance indicators:

-   Total Sales
-   Total Profit
-   Total Orders
-   Profit Margin
-   YoY Sales Growth

## Dashboard Visualizations

The dashboard contains:

-   Sales vs Target
-   Top 5 Customers by Profit
-   Sales by Category
-   Sales & Profit by State
-   Profit by Region
-   Monthly Sales Trend
-   Sales by Ship Mode
-   Sales by Sub-Category

A Region filter is included to make the dashboard interactive.

## Key Insights

Based on the dashboard:

-   Total Sales are approximately **2.30M**.
-   Total Profit is approximately **286.40K**.
-   Profit Margin is approximately **12.5%**.
-   YoY Sales Growth is approximately **46.9%**.
-   Technology is the leading sales category in the dashboard.
-   The monthly sales trend shows stronger performance toward the later
    months of the year.

## Business Recommendations

-   Focus on high-performing categories and products while protecting
    profit margins.
-   Investigate lower-profit regions and states for improvement
    opportunities.
-   Monitor discounts and their effect on profitability.
-   Use monthly sales trends for inventory and sales planning.
-   Strengthen retention of high-value customers.
